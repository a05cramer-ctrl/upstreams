How it works

No escrow, no middleman. The split lives inside pump.fun's own fee sharing.

01

Pick a repo

Any public repo, personal or org. UPSTREAM reads its contributor list from GitHub and ranks them by commits.

02

Launch with the split

Launch on pump.fun with fee sharing set to the top 9 contributors (90% by commit share) plus 10% to the $UP burn wallet.

03

They claim

Every trade pays the coin's creator fee. Each contributor claims their cut by linking their GitHub on pump.fun. Unclaimed fees wait for them.

Only the top 9 human contributors count. Accounts marked as bots are skipped.
Shares are whole percentages that always add up to 90. Nobody in the top 9 gets less than 1%.
Forks show their own contributors. Launch on the original repo to pay the people who built it.
The 10% burn slot buys $UP and sends it to a burn address.
